The spinner is missing, or nobody wants to sit out and turn it. Twister Companion takes that job: it picks the move, calls the player by name and says out loud what to do. Everybody plays.

That is what finally makes a two-player game worth it — nobody has to be the referee.

THREE WAYS TO RUN THE GAME
• By hand — tap Next when the round is over.
• Automatic — the app moves on by itself after the time you set.
• By voice — say "next", "repeat", "pause" or "resume". The phone can stay off the mat and you never have to touch it.

WHAT YOU SEE
A big coloured circle with the limb to place, sized to be read from across the room, and a countdown for the move. Names are called out loud, so nobody has to watch the screen.

FIVE GAME MODES
• Classic — Twister as you know it.
• For kids — easy moves, more time, nobody is ever out.
• Party — events all the time, for a group that came to have fun.
• Hardcore — the most awkward sequences, frequent events, half the time.
• Singing — events built around singing, with longer task time.

EVENTS
159 short tasks in three built-in packs — family, kids and party — that drop at random and turn a round upside down. You can build your own packs too, and set how often each task appears, down to a tenth of a percent.

TEN LANGUAGES
English, Polish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Turkish, Russian and Ukrainian — the interface and the spoken announcements alike.

BUILT TO BE PRACTICAL
• Works offline. No account, no sign-up, nothing to register.
• Your data stays on your phone.
• Light, dark or system theme.
• Sounds, vibrations and animations — each can be switched off on its own.
• Voice, speech rate and pitch are adjustable.

Free, with ads. A full-screen ad can appear only after a game has finished — never during play, never during a move.
